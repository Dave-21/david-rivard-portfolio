## Overview

As Lead Software Engineer on university research at Lake Superior State University (Aug 2024, May 2025), I led an engineering team building an autonomous computer vision system designed to detect, identify, and continuously track Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites in real time using small aperture optical arrays.

---

## Technical Innovations and Architecture

### 1. Computer Vision and Satellite Identification Pipeline
* Developed high speed image processing pipelines capable of isolating fast moving, low brightness point targets against astronomical star fields.
* Combined frame differencing, dynamic background subtraction, and object detection models (YOLO and OpenCV) to classify optical satellite signatures.

### 2. Orbital Trajectory Prediction
* Implemented real time orbital trajectory algorithms using Two Line Element (TLE) sets and SGP4 propagation models.
* Calculated target azimuth and elevation angles to drive automated motor mounts, ensuring continuous closed loop camera tracking across pass windows.

### 3. Edge Computing and Hardware Integration
* Integrated optical sensor arrays with Linux edge hardware and embedded controllers.
* Implemented low latency motor control loops in C++ and Python, delivering autonomous target acquisition without manual operator intervention.
