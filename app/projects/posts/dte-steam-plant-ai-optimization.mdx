## Overview

Working through Lake Superior State University in partnership with DTE Energy as a Systems Engineer (Apr 2025, Present), I architected an end to end data pipeline and optimization system for steam plant energy management.

By continuously ingesting sensor telemetry across hundreds of industrial nodes, our system accurately forecasts building heating demand, enabling plant operators to optimize energy dispatch, minimize fuel consumption, and reduce operational costs.

---

## Technical Highlights and System Architecture

### 1. Fault Tolerant Data Pipeline and High Availability Ingestion
* **Scale**: Architected a real time streaming ETL pipeline processing continuous inputs from 400+ industrial sensors (weather telemetry, building occupancy, and OCR gas meter readings).
* **Latency and Reliability**: Reduced end to end data processing latency by up to 90 seconds while maintaining 98% pipeline uptime.
* Built automatic retry policies, fallback buffers, and automated anomaly detection to eliminate sensor downtime outside major power outages.

### 2. Predictive Heating Demand Forecasting
* Researched, benchmarked, and tuned deep sequence models to generalize across seasonal weather swings:
  * Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)
  * Long Short-Term Memory networks (LSTM and BiLSTM)
  * Convolutional LSTMs (ConvLSTM)
  * Gated Recurrent Units (GRU)
* Improved overall predictive heating demand accuracy from 81% to 94%, dramatically reducing thermal over generation.

### 3. Agentic Workflow Automation and Monitoring Dashboards
* Engineered autonomous operational workflows using LangGraph and Model Context Protocol (MCP), enforcing strict guardrails and human in the loop code verification.
* Developed real time internal monitoring dashboards for plant engineers.
* Standardized CI/CD pipelines via GitHub Actions for automated model training, data validation checks, and daily operational reporting.
