## Overview

As AI Research Lead at Lake Superior State University (Aug 2024, May 2025), I architected, trained, and evaluated a custom Large Language Model from the ground up, coupled with advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) capabilities.

In addition to technical model development, I conducted independent mathematical research on Transformer attention mechanisms and presented research posters and technical demonstrations to university leadership including the University President.

---

## Key Technical Contributions

### 1. Model Architecture and Training
* Built and pre trained custom Transformer models using PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers.
* Applied Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT and LoRA) and quantization strategies to optimize memory usage during training and inference.
* Implemented tokenization and dataset token packing algorithms to maximize training throughput.

### 2. Semantic Search and RAG Vector Pipeline
* Designed a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework using dense vector embeddings and similarity search algorithms.
* Constructed automated indexing pipelines to parse, chunk, and embed structured and unstructured document repositories.
* Integrated semantic reranking to minimize hallucinations and ensure verifiable, source grounded model outputs.

### 3. Technical Communication
* Authored independent research on Transformer mathematical foundations.
* Delivered executive AI enablement presentations and live research poster sessions to university executives and faculty, driving adoption of generative AI systems across research departments.
