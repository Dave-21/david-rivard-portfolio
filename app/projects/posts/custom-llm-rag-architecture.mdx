## The Goal

Instead of just calling paid APIs, I wanted to understand how language models work under the hood by building one in code.

At Lake Superior State University (Aug 2024 - May 2025), I implemented Transformer models from scratch in PyTorch. The goal was to train small, specialized models on local GPUs and connect them to a semantic search pipeline (RAG) so the model answers technical questions using real documents instead of hallucinating.

---

## What I Built

* **Transformer from scratch**: Wrote the complete Transformer architecture in PyTorch, including multi-head self-attention and positional encodings.
* **Custom tokenizers**: Built Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) tokenizers and token-packing data loaders to pack text tightly and avoid wasting GPU memory on padding tokens.
* **Efficient fine-tuning**: Tested LoRA and QLoRA adapters with 4-bit and 8-bit quantization (bitsandbytes) to train models under strict GPU memory limits.
* **Vector search pipeline**: Built a document processing pipeline that chunks and embeds technical manuals into a vector database for similarity search.
* **Answer grounding**: Added a semantic reranking step to pull the most relevant reference passages, ensuring answers cite real source documents.
* **Faculty presentation**: Designed a research poster explaining Transformer attention mechanisms and gave live demos to university faculty and leadership.
