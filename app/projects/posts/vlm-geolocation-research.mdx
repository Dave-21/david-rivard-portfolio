## The Problem

Most geolocation models treat picking a location like a multiple-choice test:

* If a photo was taken in Paris, guessing Versailles is graded as completely wrong, the same as guessing Tokyo.
* In reality, Versailles is only 12 miles away, while Tokyo is over 5,000 miles away.

During my research at the University of Central Florida Center for Research in Computer Vision (May 2025 - Aug 2025), I worked on Geo-HiPO. The goal was to teach vision-language models how close a guess is in the real world based on actual distance, rather than just marking guesses as right or wrong.

---

## How It Works

We used preference optimization (DPO) to rank model guesses by physical distance:

* **Generate guesses**: The model predicts multiple possible locations for an image.
* **Check distance**: We turn each predicted place name into GPS coordinates and calculate how far it is from the real location.
* **Rank the guesses**: The closer guess becomes the preferred answer, and the farther guess becomes the rejected answer.
* **Step-by-step training**: The model first learns broad areas like continents and countries before training on specific cities.

## What I Built

* **30M+ image pipeline**: Built multiprocessing data loaders in Python to clean and process over 30 million images, saving over 100 hours of pipeline runtime.
* **Geocoding validation**: Set up automated checks with GeoNames and OpenStreetMap to filter out bad coordinates and fake place names before training.
* **Cluster job management**: Managed training and testing across multi-node NVIDIA H100 and V100 GPU clusters using SLURM, running sweeps of up to 50 jobs at once.
* **Fast batch inference**: Used vLLM to run high-speed batched inference on Qwen2.5-VL and Kimi-VL models to generate test predictions quickly.
* **Memory optimization**: Set up FlashAttention-2, mixed precision (AMP), and DeepSpeed so large vision models could fit on GPUs without crashing.
* **Debugging cluster issues**: Resolved GCC and CUDA version conflicts on cluster nodes and fixed token padding bugs between image features and text.

---

## Results

Tested on the standard IM2GPS3K benchmark:

* **Qwen2.5-VL-7B with HiPO** reached **35.87% accuracy within 25 km** and **54.59% within 250 km**, beating the base model (34.13% and 52.22%).
* Standard fine-tuning actually lowered accuracy to 33.97%, proving that teaching models with physical distance works much better than simple label matching.
