## Overview

During my AI Internship at the University of Central Florida Center for Research in Computer Vision (May 2025, Aug 2025), I architected and deployed large scale vision language model training and inference infrastructure on GPU computing clusters.

Our primary goal was fine tuning and evaluating vision language models on geolocation tasks, predicting geographic coordinates from ground imagery.

---

## Technical Architecture

### 1. High Throughput Distributed GPU Sweeps
* **Infrastructure**: Executed batched PyTorch and CUDA jobs across multi node V100 and H100 GPU clusters via SLURM.
* **Scale**: Managed up to 30 concurrent experiments simultaneously in week long sweeps.
* **Optimization**: Leveraged FlashAttention, PyTorch AMP, and DeepSpeed to maximize GPU compute efficiency, cutting experimental iteration timelines by weeks.

### 2. Autonomous Data Pipeline for 30M+ Images
* Automated high throughput ingestion, filtering, and curation of over 30,000,000 geographic images.
* Built preference pair dataset generation tools using Hugging Face Transformers and TRL for Supervised Fine Tuning and Direct Preference Optimization.
* Optimized data streaming using custom multiprocessing data loaders, saving over 100 hours of processing time per dataset.

### 3. Empirical Evaluation
* Wrote technical reports analyzing prediction error against physical ground truth.
* Evaluated performance using Haversine distance metrics, measuring accuracy within a 25 km radius threshold.
* Performed hyperparameter and architectural ablation studies across vision backbones, quantization regimes, and prompt formatting structures.
